DevToolkit Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 24, 2026

Publisher: mysticalg
Contact: dhookster@gmail.com

DevToolkit is an offline developer utility app. It is designed to process developer text, tokens, URLs, files, and formatting tasks locally on your device.

What the app processes
- Text, tokens, URLs, JSON, YAML, XML, regex inputs, colors, timestamps, and other content you paste, share, or type into the app.
- Small files that you explicitly pick for Base64 encoding or hashing.
- Local preferences such as theme, font, tool order, saved swatches, regex history, and saved text pipelines.
- Local tool history entries that you choose to create by using the tools.

How data is used
- All tool processing happens on-device.
- Shared text and clipboard content are only used to help you open the right tool or prefill inputs while you are using the app.
- The app does not require network access to perform its core features.

Data sharing and collection
- DevToolkit does not send your tool inputs, outputs, files, clipboard content, or history to the developer.
- DevToolkit does not sell personal data.
- DevToolkit does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Storage and retention
- History, saved swatches, regex history, saved pipelines, and preferences stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
- You can delete individual history items from the History screen.
- You can remove saved swatches, saved regex entries, and saved pipelines from their tool screens.
- The app opts out of Android cloud backup in the manifest.

Security
- The app has no backend service.
- The app requests no network permission.
- File handling is limited to files that you explicitly choose through the Android document picker.

Children and accounts
- DevToolkit does not create user accounts.
- DevToolkit does not provide account-based cloud sync.

Changes to this policy
- If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the new version will be published with a new "Last updated" date.
