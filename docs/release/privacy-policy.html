DevToolkit Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026
Publisher: mysticalg
Contact: dhookster@gmail.com

DevToolkit is an offline developer utility app. It processes developer text, tokens, URLs, formatting tasks, and selected files locally on your device.

What the app processes

  • Text, tokens, URLs, JSON, YAML, XML, regex inputs, timestamps, colours, and other content you paste, share, or type into the app
  • Small files that you explicitly choose for Base64 encoding or hashing
  • Local preferences such as theme, font, tool order, saved swatches, regex history, and saved pipelines
  • Local history entries created while you use the tools

How data is used

  • All tool processing happens on-device
  • Clipboard content and shared text are only used while you are actively using the app to prefill tools or route content to the best matching tool
  • The app does not require network access to perform its features

Data sharing and collection

  • DevToolkit does not send your tool inputs, outputs, files, clipboard content, or history to the developer
  • DevToolkit does not sell personal data
  • DevToolkit does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking

Storage and retention

  • History, saved swatches, regex history, saved pipelines, and preferences stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app
  • You can delete individual history items from the History screen
  • You can remove saved swatches, saved regex entries, and saved pipelines from their tool screens
  • The app opts out of Android cloud backup in the manifest

Security

  • The app has no backend service
  • The app declares no network permission
  • File access is limited to files that you explicitly choose through the Android document picker

Children and accounts

  • DevToolkit does not create user accounts
  • DevToolkit does not provide account-based cloud sync

Changes to this policy

If DevToolkit's data practices change, this policy will be updated and published with a new "Last updated" date.
